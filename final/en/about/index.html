About APAR

A trusted partner for premium agricultural exports from Iran to the world.

Our Story

APAR was founded with a clear vision: to bridge the gap between Iran's exceptional agricultural producers and the global market. Our founders recognized the immense potential of Iranian charcoal, legumes, and spices — products renowned for their quality but often underserved in international trade.

From our beginnings as a small trading operation, we have grown into a full-service export company, building relationships with farmers, processors, and buyers across multiple continents. Today, APAR serves clients in the Middle East, Europe, Asia, and beyond — delivering consistent quality and reliable service with every shipment.

Our Team & Experience

Behind APAR is a dedicated team of export specialists, quality control experts, and logistics professionals with deep knowledge of international trade and Iranian agriculture.

Our team brings together decades of combined experience in agricultural commodity trading, international logistics, quality assurance, and customer relations. We understand the nuances of shipping perishable and bulk goods across borders, navigating customs regulations, and meeting the diverse requirements of buyers in different markets.

From our offices in Tehran, we coordinate operations across Iran's key agricultural regions, ensuring direct sourcing, competitive pricing, and fast turnaround on orders of any size.
