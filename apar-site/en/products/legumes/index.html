Frequently Asked Questions

We export a wide range including white beans, red beans, pinto beans, black-eyed peas, chickpeas, lentils, split peas, corn, soy, mung beans, peeled wheat, and barley flakes. All products are available in retail and bulk quantities.
Minimum order quantities vary by product. For retail-packed products, minimum orders typically start at one 20ft container (~20 metric tons). For bulk orders, we can accommodate smaller quantities depending on the product. Contact us for specific MOQ information.
Yes, we provide product samples free of charge. You only cover the shipping cost. Samples are typically dispatched within 3-5 business days and allow you to verify quality, packaging, and specifications before placing a full order.
Our products comply with international food safety standards. We provide phytosanitary certificates, health certificates, and certificates of origin. Additional certifications such as ISO, HACCP, or organic can be arranged upon request.
We ship via sea freight from Bandar Abbas and Imam Khomeini ports. Transit times vary: Middle East 5-10 days, Europe 15-25 days, Asia 10-20 days, Africa 15-30 days. We handle all export documentation including bill of lading, packing list, and certificate of origin.
