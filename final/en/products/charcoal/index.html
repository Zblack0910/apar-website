Frequently Asked Questions

What types of charcoal do you export? +

APAR exports five main categories of premium charcoal: Lemon Charcoal (ideal for hookah), Chinese/Compressed Charcoal (high-heat briquettes), Pistachio Shell Charcoal (odorless, low ash), BBQ Charcoal (even heat for grilling), and Round BBQ Charcoal (designed for round grills). All products are available in various sizes and packaging configurations.

What are the minimum order quantities? +

Minimum order quantities vary by product and packaging type. For retail-packed products, MOQs typically start at 1 FCL (full container load). For bulk bags, we offer flexible quantities starting from 5 metric tons. Please contact us for specific MOQ details based on your requirements.

Do you provide samples? +

Yes, we provide product samples upon request. Samples are shipped via air freight for fast delivery so you can evaluate quality, size, burn performance, and packaging before placing a full order. Sample costs may be credited toward your first bulk order.

What certifications do your products have? +

Our charcoal products are manufactured in compliance with international quality and safety standards. Depending on the product and destination market, we can provide SGS inspection reports, ISO certifications, phytosanitary certificates, and customs documentation as required.

What are the shipping options? +

We offer FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load) sea freight options. A standard 20ft container holds 18–22 tons, and a 40ft container holds 26–30 tons. We also provide air freight for urgent or smaller orders. All shipments include proper documentation, customs clearance support, and real-time tracking.
